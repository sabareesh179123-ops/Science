1. What is Dark Energy?

Dark Energy is a mysterious, uniform form of energy that permeates all of space and exerts a repulsive gravitational force. Unlike gravity which pulls cosmic matter together, dark energy pushes space apart at an accelerating rate.

Homogeneous & Smooth: Does not clump into galaxies or stars; maintains a constant density across the universe.
Negative Pressure: Creates cosmic acceleration as dictated by Einstein’s General Theory of Relativity.

Universe Energy Composition

Based on precision observations by Planck Space Observatory & WMAP satellites:

Dark Energy ~ 68.3%
Dark Matter ~ 26.8%
Normal Baryonic Matter ~ 4.9%
Over 95% of the Universe consists of unknown dark components!

2. Key Discoveries & Researchers

1917 • Albert Einstein

The Cosmological Constant (Lambda - Λ)

Introduced into General Relativity equations to keep the static universe model balanced. Einstein later called it his "biggest blunder"—yet it laid the mathematical framework for Dark Energy.

1998 • 2011 Nobel Prize Breakthrough

Discovery of Accelerated Expansion

Saul Perlmutter (Supernova Cosmology Project) alongside Adam Riess & Brian Schmidt (High-Z Supernova Search Team) measured distant Type Ia Supernovae standard candles, proving cosmic expansion is accelerating.

2000s - Present • WMAP, Planck & DES

Precision Cosmology Validation

Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) surveys and the Dark Energy Survey (DES) mapped billions of galaxies, confirming the flat universe geometry driven by ~68% Dark Energy.

3. Leading Theoretical Hypotheses

HYPOTHESIS A

Vacuum Energy

Space naturally possesses energy. As space expands, more vacuum energy comes into existence, maintaining constant density.

HYPOTHESIS B

Quintessence

A dynamic, evolving scalar field filling space with variable energy density and state w(t) over cosmological epochs.

HYPOTHESIS C

Modified Gravity

Einstein's General Relativity might break down at gigantic intergalactic scales, requiring new gravitational laws (e.g. f(R) gravity).
